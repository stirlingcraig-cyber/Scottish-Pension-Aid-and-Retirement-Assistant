About Cairn

← Back to Cairn

Cairn is a free, independent tool for anyone trying to make sense of their pension while living in Scotland. It walks you through the questions that matter — your State Pension, workplace and public-sector pensions, tax relief, pot projections, income options and what happens to your pension when you die — and turns your answers into a plain-English summary.

Who this is for

Cairn is aimed at people who want to understand their own pension position before a conversation with a professional adviser, or who simply want a clearer picture of where they stand. It pays particular attention to points that matter under Scots law and Scottish income tax, and to Police Scotland pension arrangements, which are often poorly explained elsewhere.

What Cairn is not

Cairn does not give regulated financial advice and does not make personal recommendations. It is an educational tool, and every figure it produces is an illustration built on the assumptions you provide, not a guarantee. Before making any decision about your pension, speak to an FCA-authorised financial adviser, or use Pension Wise (free, if you're 50 or over) or MoneyHelper.

Checking who you're dealing with

If anyone contacts you unexpectedly about your pension, or pressures you to move or access it quickly, treat it as a warning sign. Check any firm on the FCA Register and use FCA ScamSmart before doing anything else.

Source and feedback

Cairn is built and maintained on GitHub. If you spot something that looks wrong, or have a suggestion, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
